Bifan Wei（魏笔凡） is a professor at the School of Continuing Education, Xi'an Jiaotong University, and a member of Shaanxi Province Key Laboratory of Big Data Knowledge Engineering. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Software and Theory from Xi'an Jiaotong University in 2014. His research focuses on knowledge engineering, big data analysis, and smart education, including knowledge graph construction, intelligent question answering, and explainable teaching plan generation.
